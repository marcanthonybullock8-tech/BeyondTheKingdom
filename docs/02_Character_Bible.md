## How to Read This Bible

- **Age (1/3/05)** is the exact age on premiere day. **Next birthday** shows the age they turn during 2005. Run `python3 tools/ages.py YYYY-MM-DD` for any date.
- **"Black Don't Crack" rule:** Every character looks noticeably younger than their age. A **Looks** line gives the on-screen appearance age range for casting and description.
- **SECRET** marks canon that is hidden from other characters, and sometimes from the audience. Nothing marked SECRET may be revealed on screen without a planned story arc.
- All principal characters in this document are African American.

## THE DONOHUE FAMILY

### Alexander Josiah Donohue — The Founder (Deceased)
- **Born:** March 14, 1905, Americus, Georgia · **Died:** December 31, 1999, 11:58 PM, Donohue Manor, Belmont Crest (aged 94)
- Founder of Donohue Global Holdings and co-founder of Belmont Crest. He was born the son of sharecroppers and walked into Atlanta at 16 with three dollars sewn into his coat lining. He built the largest fortune in human history.
- He died two minutes before the year 2000. Family legend says he "refused to see a century he didn't build."
- His 1996 **Codicil to the Crown Trust** named his great-grandson Marc-Anthony the Heir Apparent. That decision still divides the family (see Document 03).
- **Quote carved over the Manor fireplace:** *"They closed every door, so I built the house."*

### Simone Toussaint Donohue — The Queen Mother
- **Born:** November 2, 1910, New Orleans, Louisiana · **Age:** 94 · **Looks:** early 70s
- Creole of color and a licensed pharmacist (Xavier University of Louisiana, Class of 1931). Her apothecary formulas became the seed of Donohue Health & Beauty.
- Co-founder of Belmont Crest. In 1959 she stood on her porch with a shotgun while a cross burned at the front gate. The next morning she served the reporters coffee.
- She lives in the **Dowager House** at Belmont Crest. She still attends every Crown Council meeting and still has a vote. Everyone, including Victor, is afraid of her.
- **Holds:** "The Savannah Letter," the one document that proves what really happened between Alexander and Cornelius Smilley in 1952 (see Document 05).
- **Voice:** Soft Creole lilt, devastating one-liners, never raises her voice. *"Baby, I buried a husband, a century, and three governors. Don't try me before lunch."*
- **Relationship to Marc-Anthony:** He is her "Sunshine." He has Sunday dinner with her every week without fail.

### Theodore Alexander Donohue — The Passed-Over Son
- **Born:** July 31, 1934 · **Age:** 70 · **Looks:** late 50s
- Eldest son of Alexander and Simone. Howard University School of Law. Fulton County District Attorney 1971–1987, then senior counsel to Donohue Global Holdings until he retired in 1999.
- **Respected, powerful, influential, corrupt, and ruthless.** As DA he buried cases for friends, built cases against enemies, and kept files on everyone in Georgia. Those files still exist, in a climate-controlled vault under his house.
- **The wound:** In 1966 Alexander made Victor, the younger son, CEO of the newly formed Donohue Global Holdings. In 1996 he gave Victor the chairmanship too. Theodore has smiled at every family dinner for 39 years and has forgotten nothing.
- **Married:** Three times. His current wife is **Constance "Connie" Donohue** (38; see Document 07). His son is **Reginald Donohue** (47; see Document 07).
- **THE EXILE (2003–2005):**
  - In **March 2003**, Theodore sued to break the Codicil in Fulton County Superior Court. He lost.
  - Simone banished her eldest son from the Summit for **two years**.
  - Theodore, Connie, and Reginald have lived in **Monte Carlo, Monaco** ever since. Reginald runs Donohue Realty from London.
  - Ashgrove (3 Sovereign Way) sits shuttered and dark.
  - Theodore votes on the Crown Council **by written proxy only**.
- **SCREEN RULE:** Theodore and his family **do not appear on screen until Episode #176 (Monday, September 12, 2005)**, the final week of Season 1. They become **Core Contract Cast in Season 2**. Until then, the audience hears only Martin's side of his calls to "overseas."
- **SECRET:** Theodore is quietly advising Martin Olson on how to take the Heir Apparent designation away from Marc-Anthony. He plans to use Martin to put Theodore's branch of the family back on the throne.
- **Voice:** Courtroom baritone, Southern gentility, velvet threats. *"I don't hold grudges, nephew. I hold receipts."*

### Victor Donohue — The King
- **Born:** May 30, 1936 · **Age:** 68 · **Looks:** mid-50s
- Youngest son of Alexander and Simone. Morehouse College '57, Harvard Business School '59.
- **Former CEO, Donohue Global Holdings (1966–1996).** In 30 years he turned his father's Auburn Avenue businesses into the largest company in the world.
- **Chairman, Donohue Global Holdings (1996–present).**
- **Respected, powerful, influential, ruthless, dangerous, and astronomically wealthy.** He negotiates by staying silent. Senators take his calls on the first ring, and his enemies sometimes hear from him only after their companies are gone.
- **Married:** Joan Mercer on **Saturday, June 20, 1959**, in the first wedding ever held at Belmont Crest Chapel.
- **Children:** Jasmine (1960), Natasha (1961), Alvin (1970).
- **With Marc-Anthony:** Victor sees himself in his grandson and worries about that. Victor privately suspects Marc is hiding something enormous. He hasn't asked, because he isn't sure he wants the answer.
- **Voice:** Measured, low, final. *"In this family we don't raise our voices. We raise our prices."*

### The Honorable Joan Mercer Donohue — The Judge
- **Born:** April 17, 1938, Atlanta (Vine City) · **Age:** 66 · **Looks:** early 50s
- Spelman College '59. Married Victor two weeks after graduating. She was one of the few Black women in her Howard University School of Law class and graduated in 1964.
- Civil rights litigator from 1964 to 1979. She argued desegregation, voting rights, and fair-housing cases across the Deep South.
- **U.S. District Judge, Northern District of Georgia (1979–2003),** appointed by President Carter. She took senior status and retired in 2003.
- **Respected, powerful, influential, astronomically wealthy philanthropist.** She chairs the **Donohue Foundation**.
- The moral center of the family, and the only person who can make Victor apologize. She believes in the law, which puts her in quiet, permanent conflict with her husband, her son-in-law Robert, and (without her knowing it) her favorite grandson.
- **Voice:** Precise, warm, withering. *"Objection, sustained, and sit down."*

### Alvin Mercer Donohue — The Prince of Nightlife
- **Born:** October 8, 1970 · **Age:** 34 · **Looks:** late 20s
- Youngest son of Victor and Joan. He was a "surprise" baby born nine years after Natasha, and he has been spoiled rotten from the start.
- **A literal party boy.** He owns stakes in three Atlanta nightclubs (**Velvet**, **Sanctuary**, and **The Onyx Room**), a strip club he swears he doesn't own, and a Miami yacht nicknamed "The Alibi."
- **Deeply involved in politics:** He's a top Democratic fundraiser, bundles donations for city council, the state house, and Congress, and knows where every politician in Georgia drinks.
- **Deeply involved in the underworld:** He owes money and favors to **Silas Merriweather**, Atlanta's old-school kingpin (see Document 07). He launders club cash for people Victor has never heard of.
- **Sleeps around and never uses protection.** Nobody knows how many children he has, including Alvin.
  - **SECRET (seeded):** **Tremaine Banks** (12), son of **Rochelle Banks** of College Park, is Alvin's son. Rochelle has never asked for a dime. Tremaine has the Donohue eyes. More possible children are held in reserve for future stories.
- **SCREEN RULE:** Alvin has spent the past year in **Miami** on his yacht "The Alibi," dodging a federal grand jury subpoena and a debt to Silas Merriweather. He is **mentioned but never seen until Episode #90 (Monday, May 9, 2005)**, the halfway point of Season 1, when his helicopter lands on the Summit.
- **With Marc-Anthony:** Alvin is Marc's favorite uncle and a terrible influence. Marc has quietly cleaned up three of Alvin's messes, and Alvin has no idea who did it.
- **Voice:** Loud, charming, a little too drunk. *"Mama says I'm the family's black sheep. I said, Mama, I'm the family's* whole *farm."*

## THE OLSON FAMILY

### Congresswoman Jasmine Donohue Olson — The Politician
- **Born:** March 23, 1960 · **Age:** 44 · **Looks:** mid-30s
- Eldest child of Victor and Joan. Spelman '81, Georgetown Law '84.
- **U.S. Representative from Georgia (1987–present).** She was sworn in January 1987 at age 26, among the youngest women ever elected to Congress. She represents the north-metro Atlanta district that includes Belmont Crest. (Following soap convention, the district is never given a real number on screen.)
- She sits on the House Ways and Means Committee and is openly mentioned as a future Senate candidate. Privately she has vice-presidential ambitions.
- **Married:** Dr. Peter Olson, Saturday, June 14, 1980. Their marriage is a genuine love match that is also a political partnership.
- **Children:** Martin (1981), and Mallory (1985), her "miracle child." Mallory came after a devastating 1983 miscarriage and doctors telling her she would never carry again.
- **Blind spot:** She sees Martin's hunger as ambition and cannot see that it's something darker.
- **Voice:** Poised, polling-tested, lethal off-mic. *"I have 700,000 constituents, Martin. Don't make me choose."*

### Dr. Peter Olson — The Surgeon
- **Born:** November 13, 1959, Detroit, Michigan · **Age:** 45 · **Looks:** late 30s
- Son of an auto-plant foreman and a schoolteacher. He earned everything himself: University of Michigan '80, Johns Hopkins MD '84, residency and fellowship at Emory.
- **Chief of Plastic Surgery, Atlanta Presbyterian Medical Center.** Founder of **Olson Facial & Aesthetic Surgery** in Buckhead. He is dual board-certified in Plastic Surgery and Otolaryngology (ENT).
- **Respected, powerful, insanely wealthy.** Half the faces in Buckhead society are his work, and he keeps all of their secrets.
- **SECRET:** In **April 2001**, Peter was paid **$5 million in cash** to rebuild the destroyed face of an anonymous masked patient at a private clinic in Cancún. He asked no questions. The patient was **Vaughn "Silk" Harlow**, who survived the Blackwater Syndicate in 2000 (see Document 06).
- Loves Jasmine deeply and worships Mallory. Where Martin is concerned he is quietly afraid, because he once saw what Martin did to a dog when Martin was nine.
- **Voice:** Gentle Midwestern calm. *"I can fix anything but a face that lies."*

### Martin Donohue Olson — The Rival
- **Born:** May 15, 1981 · **Age:** 23 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Eldest child of Peter and Jasmine, and **the eldest grandchild of Victor Donohue**. He was born eight months before Marc-Anthony and believes that makes the throne his by right.
- Princeton '02 (economics, summa cum laude). **Senior Vice President of Strategic Development, Donohue Global Holdings.**
- **Marc-Anthony's fierce rival and sworn enemy.** Their rivalry started over toys, grew over grades, and became permanent on **Sunday, October 13, 1996**, when the Codicil was read aloud and Martin, then 15, heard his 14-year-old cousin named Heir Apparent.
- **He wants to be the Donohue Heir and take over the company.**
- **Astronomically deadly.** Martin is an expert marksman and a trained fighter who has studied poisons, and he has no conscience at all.
  - **SECRET:** Martin has killed three people as of 2005, each an "accident": a Princeton rival (1999), a blackmailing girlfriend (2002), and a DGH whistle-blower (2004). None of the deaths has been connected to him.
  - **SECRET:** He employs a private operator, **Cyrus Knox** (see Document 07), and has quietly started talks with **Desmond Smilley**, a Donohue enemy.
  - **SECRET:** Martin is hunting for the identity of "Sovereign," the boss of the Blackwater Syndicate, so he can destroy the organization and take credit for it. **He has no idea Sovereign is Marc-Anthony.** When he finds out, the war begins.
- **Voice:** Smooth, courteous, cold. *"Everyone loves Marc. That's what makes it so easy."*

### Mallory Simone Olson — The Miracle Child
- **Born:** June 5, 1985 · **Age:** 19 · **Looks:** late teens
- Youngest child of Peter and Jasmine, and their "miracle."
- Very intelligent and business-savvy. Spelman College junior (economics), taking a lighter course load to run her father's practice as **Office Manager of Olson Facial & Aesthetic Surgery**. She has grown its revenue 40% in 18 months.
- **Spoiled and a daddy's girl.** She has a black AmEx, a white Range Rover, and she calls Peter "Daddy" in board meetings.
- **Planning to launch her own business with her cousin Grace:** **"GRACE & GLORY"**, a luxury beauty and lifestyle brand of skincare, fragrance, and cosmetics built on Great-Grandmother Simone's original 1930s apothecary formulas.
- **A virgin.** Her choice, her values, her business. It's never played as a joke.
- **Story seed:** She is falling, against every rule, for **Quentin Smilley** (see Document 05), a party boy from the enemy family.
- **Voice:** Bright, fast, a little bratty. *"Daddy, I didn't* spend *the money. I* invested *it in my happiness."*

## THE BULLOCK FAMILY

### Robert Lee Bullock, Esq. — The Fixer
- **Born:** February 10, 1959, Savannah, Georgia · **Age:** 45 · **Looks:** late 30s
- Son of a longshoreman. Morehouse '80, Yale Law '83.
- **Founder & Managing Partner, Bullock & Associates, LLP (1985–present).** It's the most feared criminal-defense firm in the South, with offices in Atlanta, Miami, New York, D.C., and Los Angeles.
- **Respected, powerful, influential, astronomically corrupt, astronomically ruthless, and insanely wealthy.** He has never lost a murder trial. Rumor says that's because some witnesses never make it to trial.
- **Married:** Natasha Donohue, Saturday, September 19, 1981. They're a power couple, and more partners in crime than a romance. They still genuinely want each other.
- **Children:** Elxa (1980, born before the wedding; a family "scandal" that's now a punchline), Marc-Anthony (1982), Grace (1984).
- **SECRET:** Robert has a long-running affair with his senior partner **Camille Rhodes** (see Document 07).
- **SECRET:** In late 2004, Robert and Victor privately leaned on the mayor to make Elxa Chief of Police. **Elxa doesn't know.**
- **Voice:** Silk and gravel. *"Innocent? Son, innocent is a price point."*

### Natasha Donohue Bullock — The Empress
- **Born:** June 2, 1961 · **Age:** 43 · **Looks:** early 30s
- Middle child of Victor and Joan. Spelman '82, Wharton MBA '85.
- **CEO, Donohue Global Holdings (1996–present).** She runs the world's largest, most valuable company, a $10 trillion empire, day to day. Her personal passion is still the family's original business, the 3,100+ hotels of Donohue Hotels & Resorts.
- **Respected, powerful, influential, ruthless, dangerous, insanely wealthy.**
- **She likes to bend the rules, and her son gets it from her.** She has bribed a Monaco zoning board, blackmailed a Las Vegas regulator, and once bought a Caribbean island to win an argument.
- **With Marc-Anthony:** He's her heart and her co-conspirator. She suspects he has secrets and has decided never to ask. "A mother knows when not to know."
- **Voice:** Glamorous, blunt, amused. *"Rules are for people without lawyers. I married a lawyer."*

### Chief Elxa Bullock Jackson — The Law
- **Born:** August 29, 1980 · **Age:** 24 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Eldest child of Robert and Natasha. She was a gifted student who skipped two grades. Georgia State University B.S. in Criminal Justice (1999, age 18) and M.S. in Criminology (2001).
- Joined the **Atlanta Police Department** as soon as she met the minimum age. She rose through the ranks at a speed nobody in APD had ever seen:
  - Patrol officer (2001), then Detective in the Major Crimes/Homicide unit (2002).
  - Solved the **"Peachtree Strangler" murders** (2003) and made **Lieutenant, Homicide** (2004).
  - Her unit's clearance rate was the highest in APD history.
- **Chief of Police, Atlanta Police Department.** Appointed by Mayor Josephine Whitaker and sworn in **Monday, December 20, 2004.** She's the youngest big-city police chief in American history.
  - **Storyline:** Her appointment is a firestorm. Critics call her the "Nepotism Chief" (her father-in-law is the retiring chief and her family is the Donohues). Supporters point to her record, which is spotless.
  - **SECRET:** Her father and grandfather quietly pushed the mayor to appoint her. Elxa believes she earned it on merit alone, and her record says she did.
- **The complete opposite of her brother.** Elxa does everything **by the book**, and the book is her religion.
- **Married:** Detective Jeremy Jackson, **Saturday, October 16, 2004**, at Belmont Crest Chapel.
- **Her #1 case:** The **Blackwater Syndicate**. She has promised the city she will bring down "Sovereign." **She has no idea she's hunting her little brother.**
- **Voice:** Clipped, controlled, cop-flat. *"I don't do favors, Marc. I do warrants."*

### Marc-Anthony Bullock — The Heir Apparent
- **Born:** Tuesday, January 5, 1982, Crawford Long Hospital, Atlanta · **Age:** 22 (turns **23** on Wednesday, January 5, 2005, in Episode #0003) · **Looks:** early 20s
- Second child and only son of Robert and Natasha. Grandson of Victor and Joan. Great-grandson of Alexander and Simone.
- **Billionaire Heir and Primary Heir Apparent of the Donohue family,** named by Alexander's 1996 Codicil. Under its terms he becomes eligible to take the **Vice Chairmanship of Donohue Global Holdings on his 25th birthday (January 5, 2007)** and the Chairmanship when Victor steps down. The clock is ticking, and Martin knows it.
- **The Golden Boy of the Donohue Family.**
- **Education:**
  - Graduated Belmont Crest Academy at 12 (1994).
  - **Morehouse College**, B.S. Mathematics and B.A. Economics, 1997 (age 15).
  - **Harvard**, joint **J.D./M.B.A.**, 2001 (age 19).
- **IQ:** A reported **250**, the highest ever recorded. He has a reputation for exceptional intelligence: he reads a 400-page contract in 20 minutes, speaks nine languages, and plays chess against three people at once while roasting all of them.
- **Founder, Chairman & CEO, Blackwater Security International (BSI), 2002–present** (see Document 06).
- **SECRET (maximum clearance): Founder & Boss of the Blackwater Syndicate (2000–present), known only as "SOVEREIGN."** Only the Syndicate's inner circle, "The Seven," know his face. Arianna Cummings knows but is not a member.
- **Net worth:** Forbes' publicly estimated figure is **$38.6 billion (2005)**. The true figure, counting everything off the books, is much higher. His **future personal net worth will pass $990 billion and keep rising every single day.**
- **Personality:**
  - **The sweetest, kindest, most loving, compassionate, innocent, and pure man in the world.** He tips waiters 100%, remembers every staff member's kids' birthdays, and cries at movies.
  - **But hurt someone he loves and he becomes a monster: a dangerous, unstoppable man.** There's no middle setting.
  - **A certified crash-out.** (Writers' shorthand only: Harry Potter and Percy Jackson times 2000. In-universe, Percy Jackson won't be published until June 2005.) When he snaps, cars flip, buildings burn, and companies vanish by morning.
  - **Bends the rules and takes creative liberties** whenever the rules or laws are unjust, unfair, or ridiculous. His motto: *"The law is a floor, not a ceiling."* He's the complete opposite of his older sister Elxa.
  - **Exceptionally outgoing and charismatic.** He's a natural in the spotlight and on camera, and the tabloids love him.
  - **Natural appearance, distinctive features, and effortless charisma.**
  - **Genuinely hilarious.** His comedy is unhinged, unfiltered, and offensive, and nobody is safe, including himself and his grandfather.
- **His #1 ultimate dream:** A **family, a big, gigantic family.** Kids are **non-negotiable.** He believes in **generational wealth.**
  - **Kids love him and he loves kids.** He mentors 40 boys through the Donohue Foundation, reads to the pediatric ward at Egleston Children's Hospital every other Thursday, and is godfather to Lyric Solace Baker.
  - **SECRET (dramatic irony): He already has two daughters, twins Emma and Victoria Smilley, and has absolutely zero idea.**
- **Love interest / soulmate:** **Esther Smilley.** They have not met yet as themselves, and their story won't start right away.
  - **SECRET:** They met exactly once, masked, on the night of **Saturday, September 1, 2001**, at a Labor Day weekend masquerade (see Esther below). Marc remembers only a gold mask, a laugh, and the smell of jasmine. He has quietly looked for "the woman in the gold mask" ever since.
- **Relationships:** Ride-or-die with Grace. Brotherhood with Amond. Unbreakable friendship with Arianna. Loving, argumentative war with Elxa. Blood feud with Martin. Adores Simone, Joan, and Nana Ruby (the Bullock housekeeper). Currently on-and-off with news anchor **Celeste Vaughn** (see Document 07).
- **Residences:**
  - **The Bullock Pavilion**, 25 Sovereign Way, Belmont Crest.
  - The 58th-floor penthouse of BSI Tower in Midtown.
  - A villa in St. Barthélemy.
- **Voice:** Sweet, fast, filthy, brilliant. *"I'm the nicest man you'll ever meet. Now touch my sister again and meet the other guy."*

### Grace Natasha Bullock — The Star
- **Born:** April 12, 1984 · **Age:** 20 · **Looks:** late teens
- Youngest child of Robert and Natasha.
- **Respected and powerful actress and singer.**
  - Starred as "Keisha" on the UPN sitcom ***Sweet Tea*** (2000–2004).
  - Her debut album ***Amazing Grace*** (Oct 2004) went platinum in six weeks. The lead single is "Crown Me."
  - She's now shooting her first major studio film.
- **Planning to launch her own business with her cousin Mallory:** **GRACE & GLORY** (see Mallory).
- **Ride-or-die with her brother.** She would literally hide a body for him.
  - **SECRET:** On the night of **Saturday, July 15, 2000**, when she was 16, Marc and Amond saved her from men who drugged her at a Buckhead party. She knows the men were never seen again. She has never asked what happened. That night is the reason the Blackwater Syndicate exists (see Document 06).
- **Voice:** Sunny, sassy, fierce. *"I love my brother. I own a shovel. Those facts are unrelated."*

### Detective Jeremy Jackson — The Boy Scout
- **Born:** December 2, 1980 · **Age:** 24 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Son of **former APD Chief Harold Jackson** (see Document 07). Clark Atlanta University '02.
- **Senior Detective, APD Homicide.** He's the department's best interrogator.
- **A Boy Scout (literally an Eagle Scout) who does things 100% by the book.** He has never taken a free coffee.
- **Married:** Elxa, October 16, 2004. **Storyline:** His wife is now his boss's boss's boss. His marriage, his career, and his pride are on a collision course.
- Likes Marc personally but doesn't trust him. He is the first person to notice that the Blackwater case and the Bullock family keep crossing paths.
- **Voice:** Earnest and stubborn. *"Rules are what keep us from becoming them."*

## THE INNER CIRCLE

### Amond Baker — The Right Hand
- **Born:** February 10, 1982 · **Age:** 22 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Son of **Dr. Curtis Baker** (dentist) and **Gloria Baker** (high school principal). They're an **upper-middle-class** family in the Magnolia Terrace section of Belmont Crest (see Documents 04 and 07).
- **Marc-Anthony's best friend, brother, and right-hand man** since kindergarten at Belmont Crest Academy. Morehouse '04 (business).
- Publicly: **Senior Vice President of Strategic Operations, BSI.**
- **SECRET:** **Underboss of the Blackwater Syndicate**, "The Right Hand." He's second only to Sovereign.
- **He wants to become a respected, powerful, ruthless, dangerous, and corrupt businessman.** He grew up next to billionaires and is tired of being "the dentist's son."
- **Father of Lyric Solace Baker.**
  - Lyric was conceived in July 2001 after one night with **Harmony Divine** at a Midtown club.
  - Harmony tracked him down in February 2002, and a DNA test confirmed he's the father. Amond acknowledged Lyric immediately and pays generous support through attorneys.
  - Marc-Anthony is Lyric's godfather.
  - **But Amond and Harmony don't really know each other.** They co-parent through lawyers, nannies, and 10-minute handoffs in the Phipps Plaza parking deck.
- **Love interest:** Harmony Divine. Their story becomes an on-again, off-again, toxic, electric relationship once they finally have to talk to each other.
- **Voice:** Cool, calculating, hungry. *"Marc was born a king. I have to be made one."*

### District Attorney Arianna Cummings — The Right Hand Woman
- **Born:** October 25, 1982 · **Age:** 22 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Daughter of media mogul **Franklin Cummings** and cardiologist **Dr. Yvette Cummings**. They're an **upper-class** family on Kingsridge Drive, Belmont Crest (see Document 07).
- **Marc-Anthony's female best friend, sister, and right-hand woman.**
- **A prodigy:**
  - Spelman College at 14, B.A. at 16 (1999).
  - **Emory University School of Law**, J.D. at 18 (2001).
  - Passed the Georgia Bar in July 2001 and was **admitted October 2, 2001**, three weeks before turning 19.
- **Fulton County District Attorney.**
  - Won the November 2, 2004 election over incumbent DA **Lawrence Tate**, who collapsed after a bribery scandal broke in September.
  - Georgia requires three years of active bar membership. She qualified by exactly **31 days**.
  - **Sworn in Saturday, January 1, 2005**, two days before the premiere.
- **She wants to become Attorney General of Georgia.**
- **SECRET (maybe, maybe not):** She has skeletons in her closet that Marc helped bury.
  - **The truth:** On **New Year's Eve 2001 (early Tuesday, January 1, 2002, at about 2:40 AM)**, Arianna, 19 and drunk, hit and killed a man on a dark stretch of road off Northside Drive. She called Marc, not 911. By sunrise the car had been crushed and the body was gone. The victim was never reported found.
  - **SECRET within the secret:** The man was **Darnell "Dee" Divine**, the missing older brother of Harmony Divine. Harmony has been searching for him for three years.
  - **SECRET within the secret within the secret:** Marc also knows who tipped off the Tate scandal that got Arianna elected, because he did it.
- Arianna knows Marc is Sovereign. She's **not** a member. She protects him with plausible deniability, and he protects her with silence.
- **Voice:** Crisp, brilliant, guilty. *"Justice is blind, Marc. That's why I keep one eye open."*

## THE WOMAN IN THE GOLD MASK

### Esther Odessa Smilley — The Heiress
- **Born:** September 20, 1982, Chicago, Illinois · **Age:** 22 · **Looks:** early 20s
- **Billionaire heiress of the Smilley family.** Youngest of three children of **Lucius and Vivienne Smilley**. Full family in Document 05.
- **Her parents' designated heir** to Smilley International, chosen over her older siblings **Luke** and **Delphine**. They have never forgiven her.
- Northwestern University, B.S. (2004), and the youngest person ever admitted to Kellogg's Executive MBA program. **President, Smilley International Atlanta**, the division leading the company's January 2005 headquarters move from Chicago to Atlanta, straight into Donohue territory.
- **1000% ruthless and corrupt.** She'll bribe, blackmail, seduce, sabotage, and smile the whole time.
- **The most beautiful and attractive woman in the world, all natural.** She's been on the cover of *Vogue*, *Essence*, *Ebony*, *Jet*, and *Forbes*.
- **Her best friend and sister:** Harmony Divine, her freshman roommate at Northwestern.

## Who They Are

The Smilleys are **Chicago's Black royalty.** The Donohues are Southern old money: land, faith, legacy. The Smilleys are Northern new money turned old: **glamour, jazz, fashion, and velvet cruelty.** The Donohues build kingdoms. The Smilleys build **brands.**

They are the **second-wealthiest family in the world** and the Donohues' **fiercest rivals and competitors.** The two families have been at war since the **Savannah Betrayal of March 7, 1952** (see Document 03). In **January 2005**, the Smilleys move their headquarters from Chicago to Atlanta, into Donohue territory.

## THE FOUNDER

### Cornelius Augustus Smilley (Deceased)
- **Born:** Saturday, August 8, 1908, Vicksburg, Mississippi · **Died:** Thursday, February 19, 1987, Chicago (aged 78)
- Left Mississippi in 1925 during the Great Migration. Worked as a **Pullman porter from 1926 to 1937** and was a proud member of the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters.
- Pullman porters were forced to answer to "George" and to smile for tips. Cornelius smiled for eleven years. **The family name was originally "Smiley."** He added the second "L" in 1938 when he opened his first hotel: *"They made me smile for them. Now they'll pay to see me smile, and they'll spell it my way."*
- A brilliant showman who treated **hotels as theater.** Doormen in white gloves, a jazz orchestra every night, and fresh roses in every room.
- **His wound:** The Savannah Betrayal (1952). He died believing Alexander Donohue robbed him.
- **Died** of a heart attack the day after the Donohue Chicago opened directly across from his flagship. The Smilleys call it murder by hotel.

## THE FAMILY

### Odessa Hart Smilley — The Dowager Empress
- **Born:** January 24, 1920, Chicago (Bronzeville) · **Age:** 84 (turns 85 on Monday, January 24, 2005) · **Looks:** mid-60s
- A former torch singer at the Regal Theater and the Smilley Hotel's supper club, the Crystal Room. She married Cornelius in 1946. He was 37 and she was 26.
- **Simone Donohue's nemesis.** The two queens met exactly once, at a 1963 NAACP gala. Their 90-second conversation is still discussed in Chicago and Atlanta.
- She's convinced Simone holds proof of what really happened in Savannah. **She's right.**
- Named Esther's middle name ("Odessa"). **She suggested "Simone" as Victoria's middle name,** saying it was after "a French jazz singer I once loved." It was really a private dig at her rival. She has no idea the baby is Simone's great-great-granddaughter.
- **Voice:** Gravelly, grand, cruel. *"Simone Donohue has been ninety years old since 1950."*

### Lucius Cornelius Smilley — The Chairman
- **Born:** November 30, 1949 · **Age:** 55 · **Looks:** early 40s
- Howard University '71, University of Chicago MBA '74. **Chairman & CEO of Smilley International (1987–present).**
- Charming, cultured, and vindictive. He grew Smilley International from $40 billion to $1.9 trillion. He has hated the Donohues since he was two years old, because he was raised to.
- **Moving the headquarters to Atlanta** is his life's crusade: *"Daddy died looking at their hotel. I'll die looking at their grave."*
- **Named Esther his heir** on her 21st birthday (**Saturday, September 20, 2003**) at the Smilley Harlem gala, passing over Luke and Delphine. Luke threw a champagne glass at the ice sculpture.
- **SECRET:** Lucius was diagnosed with **early-onset Parkinson's disease** in 2003. Only his neurologist and Vivienne know. It's why he rushed the succession, and he hides a tremor behind a cane he claims is "for style."
- **SECRET:** He has long suspected that Luke is not his biological son.
- **Voice:** Velvet baritone, literary, poisonous. *"We don't lose to the Donohues. We* allow *them to finish second, graciously, for now."*

### Vivienne Beaumont Smilley — The Brand
- **Born:** March 17, 1955, Washington, D.C. (the Gold Coast neighborhood) · **Age:** 49 · **Looks:** mid-30s
- A former **Ebony Fashion Fair** runway model, and the first Black woman to front a major French perfume campaign (1976).
- **Chief Brand Officer, Smilley International.** She created the **Smile** fragrance, the ***Odessa*** couture line, and **Hart Home**.
- An icy perfectionist who loves her children in order of usefulness.
- **SECRET:** **Luke is Desmond's son.** It came from a two-month affair with her brother-in-law in the spring of 1975. Desmond knows. Lucius suspects. Luke has no idea.
- **Voice:** Cool, elegant, surgical. *"Darling, I don't dislike you. I simply don't see you."*

### Desmond Hart Smilley — The Snake
- **Born:** August 21, 1956 · **Age:** 48 · **Looks:** late 30s
- Lucius's younger brother. **Vice Chairman, Smilley International.** He runs the real estate division, **Smilley Estates**.
- Divorced (twice). Father of Quentin. **Secretly Luke's biological father.**
- Has resented being the "spare" his whole life. He plans to use Luke's claim, and then reveal Luke's true paternity, to push Esther out and seize the company.
- **SECRET:** He is in secret talks with **Martin Olson**, an enemy inside the enemy's house. Their deal: help each other remove the two heirs, Esther and Marc-Anthony.
- **Voice:** Easy laugh, dead eyes. *"Family is everything. That's why I keep a copy of everyone's."*

### Lucius "Luke" Smilley Jr. — The Dethroned Prince
- **Born:** February 2, 1976 · **Age:** 28 · **Looks:** mid-20s
- Eldest child. Morehouse '98 (the Smilleys chose Atlanta for college as a statement), Harvard MBA '02.
- **President, Smilley Hotels & Resorts.** He's genuinely good at hotels.
- He was raised as the heir until 2003, and has been **humiliated and furious** ever since.
- **Married:** Noelle Price, May 26, 2001. They've been through years of failed fertility treatments, which makes Esther's "IVF twins" feel like a daily insult.
- **Doesn't know Desmond is his biological father.**
- **Voice:** Loud, entitled, wounded. *"I did everything right. She did everything* first."

### Noelle Price Smilley — The Social Climber
- **Born:** June 1, 1978, Milwaukee · **Age:** 26 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Former **Miss Black Wisconsin (1998)** and a Chicago TV weather anchor. Luke's wife.
- Desperate for a baby, a title, and a seat at the Belmont Crest Country Club. **She'd betray the Smilleys in a heartbeat for the Donohues' approval.**
- **Voice:** Sweet as pie and twice as fattening. *"I'm not jealous, I'm just paying attention."*

### Delphine Smilley-Ashe — The Ice Queen
- **Born:** October 5, 1979 · **Age:** 25 · **Looks:** early 20s
- Middle child. Parsons School of Design '01. **President, Maison Smilley**, the luxury department stores and lifestyle brands.
- A fashion icon and a *Vogue* favorite, with a taste for elegant revenge. She hates Esther with style.
- **Married:** **Gideon Ashe**, June 21, 2003, at the Smilley Harlem.
- **Voice:** Clipped, bored, lethal. *"Esther got Daddy's company. I got his taste. We'll see which lasts."*

### Gideon Ashe — The Gambler
- **Born:** July 18, 1975, New York · **Age:** 29 · **Looks:** mid-20s
- Founder of **Ashe Capital**, a hedge fund. He's handsome and smooth, and he's lying about everything.
- **SECRET:** The fund is collapsing, and Gideon owes **$212 million** to underground high-stakes card games. **Those games are run by the Blackwater Syndicate.** Sovereign holds the Smilley son-in-law's marker, and Marc doesn't yet realize what leverage he has.
- **Voice:** Easy confidence, constant sweating. *"It's a liquidity event, not a problem."*

### Esther Odessa Smilley — The Heiress
*(Full profile in Document 02.)*
- **Age:** 22. **Designated heir. President, Smilley International Atlanta.** She personally runs the move to Atlanta.
- **Her masterstroke:** In 2002 she began building a 72-story tower in Buckhead under a shell company called **Peachtree Pinnacle Partners**. Nobody knew who owned it until the December 2004 announcement. **It was the same trick Alexander Donohue used to buy Belmont Crest in 1955.** Victor noticed. Simone laughed out loud.
- **Mother of Emma and Victoria** ("the IVF twins").

### Quentin Hart Smilley — The Wild Card
- **Born:** November 11, 1983 · **Age:** 21 · **Looks:** late teens
- Desmond's only child. He dropped out of Northwestern and is a DJ and club promoter who plans to "go to Atlanta and run the city."
- **Alvin Donohue's new best friend** in the club scene. **Mallory Olson's forbidden attraction.** He's sweeter than he pretends to be, and far less stupid.
- He has no idea he's his father's pawn.
- **Voice:** Charming, clowning, and secretly lonely. *"I'm not the black sheep. I'm the sheep that brought the party."*

### Emma & Victoria Smilley — "The IVF Twins"
*(Full profiles in Document 02.)* Both are 2. Their nanny is **Beatrix Pennington** (Document 07). The Smilleys adore them. Their grandfather Lucius calls them "my dividends." **Nobody in the family knows whose they are.**

## THE HISTORY OF SMILLEY INTERNATIONAL

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1938** | Cornelius opens **The Smilley**, a 90-room hotel on South Parkway in Bronzeville, with the **Crystal Room** supper club. Nat King Cole, Billie Holiday, and Count Basie perform there. |
| **1941–1951** | Green Book expansion across the North and Midwest: **The Smilley Harlem** (1946), Detroit, Cleveland, St. Louis, Kansas City, and Pittsburgh. Plus a lakeside resort at **Idlewild, Michigan**, the "Black Eden." |
| **March 7, 1952** | **The Savannah Betrayal.** The Donohue-Smilley merger collapses. |
| **1953–1960** | **Smilley Realty** is founded. During the era of predatory "contract buying," it builds and finances fair-mortgage apartment buildings for Black Chicagoans, and becomes Chicago's largest Black landlord. |
| **1962** | Smilley Resorts opens at **Oak Bluffs, Martha's Vineyard** and in **Montego Bay, Jamaica**. |
| **1968** | South Parkway is renamed Martin Luther King Drive. Cornelius keeps the flagship's lights on for 30 straight days in mourning. |
| **1974–1979** | Smilley Resorts expands to Barbados, Nassau, and the Riviera. **The Smilley at Lake Shore**, a 60-story luxury condominium tower, opens on Chicago's Gold Coast in 1979. |
| **1983** | **Maison Smilley**, the first Black-owned luxury department store on the Magnificent Mile, opens at 900 N. Michigan Avenue. |
| **Wednesday, February 18, 1987** | **Donohue Chicago** opens directly across from The Smilley. |
| **Thursday, February 19, 1987** | Cornelius dies of a heart attack the next morning. **Lucius, 37, becomes Chairman & CEO.** |
| **1989** | The company is renamed **Smilley International, Inc.**, and goes global with hotels in London (Mayfair), Paris, Dubai, and Monaco. |
| **1990s** | **Lifestyle brands:** Smile fragrance (1991), *Odessa* couture (1994), Hart Home (1997). Maison Smilley flagships open in New York, Beverly Hills, London, Paris, and Tokyo. |
| **2001** | Smilley Estates builds branded luxury residences in 20 cities. |
| **September 20, 2003** | **Esther is named heir.** |
| **2002–2004** | "Peachtree Pinnacle Partners" secretly builds a 72-story tower in Buckhead. |
| **Thursday, December 9, 2004** | **The Announcement:** Smilley International will move its world headquarters to **Smilley Tower, 3434 Peachtree Road NE, Atlanta, GA 30326**, directly across Peachtree Road from Donohue Tower. |
| **Monday, January 3, 2005** | **Smilley Tower officially opens** on the morning of the series premiere. |

## THE FEUD — Rules of Engagement

1. **Every Donohue opening gets a Smilley counter-opening,** usually within the same month.
2. **Neither family has attended the other's funeral since 1952.**
3. Hospitality is the main battlefield: Donohue Hospitality Group vs. Smilley Hotels & Resorts. Natasha vs. Luke. Natasha vs. Esther.
4. **2005 battlegrounds:**
   - The **Atlanta skyline** (the rival towers)
   - The **Belmont Crest Country Club** (Noelle wants in)
   - **Harmony's brokerage** (Esther steers Smilley deals to her best friend)
   - **The Olympic-sized fight over the "Auburn Avenue Revival" redevelopment bid.** Whoever wins it rebuilds the street where Alexander started.
5. **Hidden alliances:** Martin + Desmond. Noelle + anyone. Gideon and his debt to Blackwater. Mallory + Quentin.

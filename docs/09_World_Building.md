# BEYOND THE KINGDOM — World Building

**Document 09 · Locked Canon · The World, the Country, Georgia & Atlanta (1905 – January 3, 2005)**

---

## PART 1 — THE RULES OF THIS UNIVERSE

1. **"The Real World, Plus."** *Beyond the Kingdom* takes place in the real United States of 2005, with the real President, the real wars, the real weather, and the real songs, plus a layer of fictional families, companies, and institutions.
2. **Real history stays real.** September 11, the Iraq War, the Indian Ocean tsunami, and Hurricane Katrina all happen on their real dates. The show reacts to them respectfully and never exploits them.
3. **Fictional officeholders (soap convention):**
   - Real public officials below the national level are **never named or impersonated**.
   - Atlanta's Mayor (Josephine Whitaker), the APD Chief (Elxa Jackson), the Fulton County DA (Arianna Cummings), and Jasmine Olson's congressional district are fictional.
   - The Governor and the state Attorney General are referred to only by title.
   - National figures (the President, for example) exist in the background but are never shown doing anything the real person didn't do.
4. **No supernatural elements.** Every twist has to be possible, even if it's outrageous. This is soap realism: big feelings, real consequences.
5. **Black excellence is the baseline, not the exception.** In this world, the two richest families on Earth are Black. The show treats that as history that happened, not as fantasy.
6. **Continuity is sacred.** Anything that has aired is permanent.

## PART 2 — THE COUNTRY: THE UNITED STATES, JANUARY 2005

### Politics
- **President George W. Bush** won re-election on **November 2, 2004**, and his **second inauguration is Thursday, January 20, 2005** (a preempted airdate). The country is bitterly divided after the 2004 election.
- **The 109th Congress convenes Tuesday, January 4, 2005.** Congresswoman Jasmine Olson (D-GA), the Donohues' eldest daughter, is sworn in for her **10th term** that day (Episode #2).
- **The Iraq War** is nearly two years old, and **Afghanistan** is in its fourth year. Military families and veterans are a real presence in Atlanta. BSI hires veterans aggressively.
- **Homeland security** is a boom industry after September 11, 2001. That boom made **BSI's** meteoric rise possible (2002–2005).

### The Economy
- The **housing boom** is at its peak. Easy mortgages, flipping, and luxury condos are everywhere. It's the perfect moment for Harmony to become a broker, and for Smilley Estates to invade Atlanta.
- Gas averages about **$1.80 a gallon**. It will spike after Katrina.
- Wall Street is recovering from the dot-com bust. The Donohues bought the bottom (2000–2002).

### Technology (what people actually have in 2005)
- **Phones:** Flip phones (the **Motorola RAZR** is *the* status phone), **BlackBerrys** for executives, **T-Mobile Sidekicks** for the young and fabulous, and two-way pagers. Texting is new and addictive. **No smartphones, no iPhone (2007), no apps.**
- **Music:** iPods, burned CDs, ringtones.
- **Internet:** Dial-up is dying and broadband is spreading. People use AOL Instant Messenger (AIM), MySpace, BlackPlanet, LiveJournal, Xanga, Blogger, message boards, and email forwards. **Facebook is limited to college students. YouTube launches this year. Twitter and Instagram don't exist.**
- **TV:** Cable news, BET, UPN (*Girlfriends*, and in this universe Grace's *Sweet Tea*), *Chappelle's Show*, *American Idol*. DVRs (TiVo) are new.

### Culture — The Soundtrack of 2005
- **Atlanta runs hip-hop and R&B:** OutKast, Usher, Ludacris, T.I., Ciara, Lil Jon and the crunk movement, and Jermaine Dupri's So So Def. The Atlanta sound is everywhere.
- Kanye West's *The College Dropout* is the album of the moment.
- In film, Jamie Foxx is winning everything for *Ray*, and Atlanta's own Tyler Perry is about to open *Diary of a Mad Black Woman* (Friday, February 25, 2005).
- **In this universe:** Grace Bullock's platinum album *Amazing Grace* (October 2004) and her single "Crown Me" are real chart hits.

### Sports
- The **Atlanta Falcons** won the NFC South. They beat the Rams in the playoffs on **Saturday, January 15, 2005**, and lose the NFC Championship to the Eagles on **Sunday, January 23, 2005**. That's great background texture for January episodes.
- The Super Bowl is **Sunday, February 6, 2005**, in Jacksonville.

## PART 3 — THE DIVERGENCE: HOW THE DONOHUES, SMILLEYS & BLACKWATER CHANGED THE WORLD

### "The Donohue Economy"
- **Donohue Global Holdings is worth $10 trillion**, roughly a fifth of the entire world economy. Its scale makes it a quasi-sovereign power:
  - Victor Donohue is invited to G8 summits as an "observer."
  - Presidents of both parties call him on the first ring.
  - Economists track the **"Donohue Index"**: when DGH moves capital, markets move with it.
- The **Donohue Foundation** gives away more than $30 billion a year. **In this universe, HBCUs are among the best-endowed universities in America.** Spelman, Morehouse, Howard, and Xavier compete with the Ivy League for students and faculty, thanks to decades of Donohue money.
- **The Donohue Code of Conduct:** DGH's labor standards (the highest pay floor in every industry it touches) have quietly raised wages worldwide. Unions love Joan. Rivals hate Victor.

### The Smilley Counterweight
- **Smilley International ($1.9 trillion)** is the world's second-most-valuable private company. It is glamour to the Donohues' gravity. Its move to Atlanta in January 2005 is called **"the Second Great Migration of money."**
- **Cultural divide:** Team Donohue (old Southern legacy, faith, discretion) versus Team Smilley (Northern glamour, fashion, flash). Black America debates it at barbershops, beauty salons, and Sunday dinners.

### Blackwater
- **BSI** has made private security a respectable, even beloved, industry in America, especially after its free disaster-relief deployments.
- **The Blackwater Syndicate** has quietly changed the underworld. Human trafficking in the Southeast has collapsed by 80%, because "Sovereign" hunts traffickers. Law enforcement can't prove any of it.
- **"Who is Sovereign?"** is a national parlor game, the way "Who shot J.R.?" once was.

### Black Wealth in This Universe
- There are "two Americas" of Black wealth. There's the **Donohue and Smilley stratosphere** (families worth hundreds of billions). Then there's the thriving Black professional class beneath it: the Bakers, the Cummingses, the Divines. That class exists in real life, and the show honors it.
- **The show never forgets the middle class.** Harmony, Calvin, and Loretta Divine remind everyone how the other 99% of Atlanta lives.

## PART 5 — ATLANTA, 2005: "THE BLACK MECCA"

### The City
- The **city** has roughly 450,000 people, and the **metro** area nearly 5 million.
- **Hartsfield-Jackson** is the busiest airport on Earth.
- Atlanta is home to **Spelman, Morehouse, Clark Atlanta, and Morris Brown** (the Atlanta University Center), the **King Center**, **Ebenezer Baptist**, and **Sweet Auburn**.
- In this universe, Atlanta is also the world headquarters of the **two most valuable companies on Earth**. The Buckhead skyline is defined by the rival towers: **Donohue Tower** (88 stories) and **Smilley Tower** (72 stories), which face each other across Peachtree Road.
- Atlanta's nickname in-universe: **"The Capital of Black Royalty."**

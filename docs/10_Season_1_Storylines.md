# BEYOND THE KINGDOM — Season 1 Storylines

**Document 10 · Locked Canon · Season 1: "The Arrival" (Episodes #0001–#0180, January 3 – September 16, 2005)**

---

## The Season in One Sentence
**When the Smilleys plant their tower across Peachtree Road from the Donohues, Atlanta's Black royalty goes to war, and the secrets buried under Belmont Crest (a masked night, a missing man, a dead man who isn't dead, and a king nobody can see) begin to surface, one by one, until they explode at a wedding.**

## A-STORIES (Tentpole Arcs)

### 1. "THE WOMAN IN THE GOLD MASK" — Marc & Esther
**Logline:** Heirs to rival dynasties fall in love while one of them hides that they already share two daughters.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **The father meets his daughters before he formally meets their mother.** Marc bonds with Victoria at Lenox Square (#18), rescues her again when she crosses the ravine (#91), and plays the dragon at Lyric's party (#77), all before he knows. The audience watches a father fall in love with his children in real time while he has no idea.
- **Beats:**
  - Near-misses: the Crown Gate (#3), the jasmine at church (#11), the adjoining rooms (#33)
  - First meeting (#42–43)
  - Three dances at the Centennial Ball (#50)
  - The library standoff (#68)
  - "She's got my laugh" (#92)
  - **First kiss, in the rain on the Hotel Donohue roof (100th episode)**
  - Celeste's kiss sends Esther away (#105)
  - The motel confession: he's been searching for the gold-mask girl for four years (#157)
  - Katrina brings them together (#170–171)
  - "Give me one reason" (#178)
  - **Finale: "The girls are yours!" (#180)**
- **Season 2 question:** Did Marc hear her?

### 2. "WHO IS SOVEREIGN?" — The Hunt
**Logline:** The Chief of Police, the FBI, a star reporter, and a murderous cousin all hunt Atlanta's invisible crime king, and he's at their Sunday dinner.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **The Sovereign Broadcast (#38).** The villain-hero hijacks a live local newscast to promise the police chief, who is secretly his sister, a killer delivered to her door in seven days, and then keeps the promise (#42).
- **Beats:**
  - The Onyx Coin killings frame Blackwater (#1–#23)
  - "SOVEREIGN = BULLOCK": Robert is questioned (#23–24)
  - Celeste's exposé ends her relationship with Marc (#26)
  - The countdown (#38–#42)
  - Martin's investigation: Harlow tells him "two boys came" (#149), Martin stages a test (#165), and **he knows (#180)**
  - Doyle learns Robert is Sovereign's lawyer (#146)
  - Hughes leaks Amond as a target (#161)
  - Harmony wears a wire (#162–163)

### 3. "THE GHOST OF DEE DIVINE" — Harmony, Arianna, Marc & Amond
**Logline:** A woman searching for her missing brother turns to the new DA for help, not knowing the DA killed him and her best friend's secret love buried the truth.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **The killer is the one assigned to solve the case.** Harmony begs Arianna to reopen it (#4) and hires Marc's company to search for Dee (#40), so the two people who buried him are officially looking for him.
- **Beats:**
  - The flashback special reveals the truth to the audience (#35)
  - Esther's investigators find Northside Drive (#64), and Esther uses it against Arianna (#67)
  - Arianna writes a confession letter, and Knox steals it (#117–118). Martin blackmails her (#120).
  - Silas mails Dee's wallet (#134), and the Divines learn Dee is dead (#135)
  - Amond learns Blackwater delivered a body (#136) and punches Marc into the Chattahoochee (#137)
  - A funeral without a body (#139)
  - Harmony confronts Arianna, and Martin interrupts (#142)
  - **The mailed confession arrives on the wedding day (#179–180)**

### 4. "HOUSE OF SMILLEY" — The Enemy's Civil War
**Logline:** The Smilleys' move into enemy territory unravels their own family: a secret illness, a secret father, and an heir nobody wanted.
- **Beats:**
  - Luke and Delphine's resentment (#7, #16)
  - Noelle steals the Savannah Letter (#54–55)
  - Lucius collapses (#94) and his Parkinson's is leaked (#95)
  - Desmond tells Martin "Luke is mine" (#96). Delphine finds the 1975 letters (#97) and throws the truth at Luke (#101).
  - **Lucius hears everything from his hospital bed (#102)** and rewrites the succession (#103)
  - Desmond and Luke join Martin's alliance (#104)

## B-STORIES

### 5. "THE TOWER WAR" — The Auburn Avenue Revival
**Logline:** DGH and Smilley International fight over the $4 billion redevelopment of Sweet Auburn, where Alexander Donohue began.
- **Never-before-seen twist:** Esther wins by bribing a councilman (#98). Marc answers with the deeds to Alexander's original 31 parcels, and **turns her victory into a forced partnership** (#99–100). The rivals become business partners without a single handshake.

### 6. "LYRIC'S LAW" — Amond & Harmony
**Logline:** Two strangers who share a daughter are forced into one penthouse by danger, and into each other's arms by everything else.
- **Beats:**
  - Gloria's grandparent-visitation petition is served (#1–#2). Harmony refuses the one-Sunday compromise (#3), and the court orders a hearing (#9).
  - The evaluator (#17)
  - The nursery break-in (#29) and moving into the penthouse (#30)
  - Sleeping together after the ball (#52)
  - The Grady night (#116)
  - The Juneteenth kiss (#119)
  - Amond's break with Marc (#137)
  - The wire (#162–163)
  - **She reads the confession and looks at him (#180)**

### 7. "THE NEPOTISM CHIEF" — Elxa & Jeremy
**Logline:** A by-the-book police chief learns her appointment was bought by the family she refuses to owe.
- **Beats:**
  - Hughes' leaks (#9)
  - The no-confidence hearing and the Hardaway letter (#72)
  - Jeremy hides the truth (#73) and finally tells her (#108)
  - The resignation letter (#108), which the mayor refuses (#110)
  - A pregnancy revealed in an evidence bag (#160)
  - Katrina hits on her forgotten 25th birthday (#168)

### 8. "GRACE UNDER FIRE" — Grace, Derrick & the Monster
**Logline:** A pop star hides her abuse from her family until her brother sees one photograph.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **The hero's arrest is the sweeps climax.** Marc flips the abuser's Bentley in the middle of Peachtree Road, and **his own sister, the Chief, arrests him (#84).** That arrest triggers the Disgrace Clause vote, which **Alvin, returning from Miami, decides with his first on-screen act (#90).**

### 9. "GRACE & GLORY" — The Beauty War
**Logline:** Two heiresses build a brand on their great-grandmother's 1935 formulas, until the enemy copies them.
- **Beats:**
  - Simone's condition: "Beat the Smilleys" (#4)
  - The formula is copied as "Odessa Glow" (#62)
  - The lawsuit shows the leak was a bribed chemist (#112)
  - **The launch sells out in 11 minutes (#145)**

### 10. "FORBIDDEN FRUIT" — Mallory & Quentin
**Logline:** Romeo and Juliet in Buckhead: the Olsons' miracle child and the Smilleys' wild card.
- **Beats:**
  - "Q" (#8)
  - The sunflower (#13)
  - The gazebo kiss (#51)
  - Desmond orders Quentin to spy (#61)
  - The confession, and the gift thrown into Lake Simone (#111)
  - **Quentin saves her from a sabotaged car (#126)** and defies his father (#127)
- Mallory's choice to wait is treated with respect. Quentin's love is proven by his patience.

### 11. "THE MAN WITH A NEW FACE" — Harlow Returns
**Logline:** The monster Blackwater thought it destroyed in 2000 comes back wearing a face built by a Donohue son-in-law.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **The villain's face was sculpted by a member of the hero's family.** Peter unknowingly created the disguise that lets Harlow into Belmont Crest society, and Alvin unknowingly gives him a nightclub.
- **Beats:**
  - The anonymous photos (#13, #57)
  - "You gave me a new life" (#74)
  - The Cancún flashback (#75)
  - The threat to Mallory (#76), and her brakes fail (#126)
  - Marc sees his eyes (#128)
  - The white rose (#130)
  - The fingerprint (#132), which is thrown out (#133)
  - "Two boys came" (#149)
  - **He walks the wedding grounds as a florist (#175)**
  - **The gunfire (#180)**

### 12. "THE PRODIGAL PRINCE" — Alvin (from Episode #90)
**Logline:** The family's black sheep lands in the middle of a family civil war, and brings a wolf with him.
- **Beats:**
  - His helicopter entrance and swing vote (#90)
  - The Sunday roast and Silas's funeral wreath (#93)
  - The bender with Harlow, where he leaks Grace's secret (#115)
  - Defending "Julian" (#133)
  - Coming face to face with his unknown son Tremaine (#159)

### 13. "KINGS AND PAWNS" — Martin's Conspiracy
**Logline:** The passed-over grandson assembles a secret alliance of enemies to destroy the Heir Apparent, guided by a voice from exile.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** For 175 episodes, **the audience hears only Martin's side of his calls.** The identity of the "Voice" is the season's slow-burn mystery, solved when **Theodore Donohue returns (#176).**
- **Beats:**
  - The Desmond pact (#8)
  - Knox and the Onyx killings (#10–#41)
  - The Disgrace petition (#85), which fails at the swing vote (#90)
  - The alliance widens to Desmond, Luke, Knox, and Harlow (#104, #148)
  - Arianna's letter (#118)
  - **The DNA test: 99.99% (#166–173)**
  - "Come home. It's time." (#173)

### 14. "THE OTHER WOMAN WEARS A WIRE" — Robert, Natasha & Camille
**Logline:** A wife discovers her husband's mistress and hires her, and the mistress turns out to be working for the FBI.
- **Beats:**
  - The Smile perfume (#6)
  - Natasha buys the hotel where they meet (#32)
  - Natasha hires Camille as DGH's General Counsel at triple salary (#65)
  - The Bullocks reunite as partners in crime (#107)
  - Camille wears the wire (#114) and catches "Sovereign's people pay" (#146)
  - She's discovered: "I'm pregnant. It's his." (#147)
  - The grand jury (#175)

## C-STORIES & RUNNERS

### 15. "THE SAVANNAH LETTER" — Simone & Odessa
**Logline:** Two matriarchs in their 80s and 90s discover the truth behind their families' 53-year war, and decide to keep it to themselves.
- **Never-before-seen hook:** **Daytime's first road trip between two elderly queens.** Simone and Odessa escape their security and drive to Savannah together in a 1958 Cadillac (#154–157). Their grandchildren chase them, and it traps the lovers in a one-room motel.
- **Beats:**
  - The Centennial Ball face-off (#50)
  - The theft (#50–55)
  - Tea at the Swan Coach House (#63)
  - They read the letter together: "It was always me." (#123)
  - The secret pact (#124)

### 16. "THE CONGRESSWOMAN'S GAMBIT" — Jasmine & Peter
Jasmine's 2006 run for governor (#57, #124) collides with Peter's secret surgery on Harlow (#125). Her public ambition stays blind to Martin's private darkness.

### 17. "THE UNKNOWN DONOHUE" — Tremaine
Marc spots Victor's eyes on a boy in his mentoring program (#113). Alvin meets his son (#159). This runner gets a full arc in Season 2.

### 18. "THE STORM" — Katrina
- **Real-event arc, handled with respect.** BSI Crisis Response and Blackwater's Tithe pour into New Orleans, Simone's hometown.
- Esther opens every Smilley hotel to evacuees, the rival heirs run a joint relief center, and Simone's great-nephew is rescued.
- **Every Atlanta shelter is funded anonymously with an Onyx Coin (#172)**, and Sovereign becomes a national legend.

### 19. "THE RETURN OF THE EXILES" — Theodore's Branch (#176–180)
The two-year exile ends. Theodore, Reginald, and Connie arrive in finale week. The "Voice" is revealed, Connie is revealed to be CrestWatch (#179), and an emergency Crown Council is called for October. **They become Core Contract Cast in Season 2.**

## THE SEASON FINALE — "The Wedding" (Episode #180, Friday, September 16, 2005)

**Cliffhangers (the Season 2 questions):**
1. **Who was shot?** Blood spreads across Esther's gown. Is it Marc's, Esther's, or one of the twins'?
2. **Did Marc hear "The girls are yours"?**
3. **Harmony has Arianna's confession.** What will she do, and how much does Amond know?
4. **Martin knows everything.** He knows Sovereign's identity and the twins' paternity, and he has Theodore at his side.
5. **Was Harlow the shooter, or was someone else in the trees?**
6. **Did Esther say "I do"?**
7. **Camille's grand jury testimony and her baby.** Can Robert survive either one?
8. **The October Crown Council.** Theodore is back for the crown.

## How the Categories Work (Real Daytime Practice)

| Category | What It Means | Credits | Typical S1 Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core / Contract Cast** | The headliners. Multi-year contracts with a guaranteed minimum number of episodes per 13-week cycle. They carry the A-stories. | Opening titles | 80–160 |
| **Featured Contract Cast** | On contract, with a lower guarantee. They carry B-stories and step up during their arcs. | Opening titles | 40–80 |
| **Recurring** | Not on contract, but booked regularly across the season | Closing credits | 15–60 |
| **Supporting** | Named characters who appear occasionally when a story needs them | Closing credits | 3–16 |
| **Day Players** | One-day or short-arc roles, often "under-fives" (fewer than five lines) | Closing credits | 1–5 |
| **Guest Stars** | Special or limited appearances: flashbacks, returns, event cameos, and characters being introduced ahead of a promotion to contract | "Special Guest Star" billing | 1–10 |

**The rotation rule:** Being a main character **does not** mean appearing in every episode. A typical week features 3–5 storylines. Core players usually work 2–4 episodes a week, then disappear for a week while another story gets the spotlight. Even Marc-Anthony, the show's center, sits out about 1 in 8 episodes.

**Scheduling rules:**
- **Alvin Donohue** first appears in **Episode #90 (Monday, May 9, 2005)**, the halfway point of Season 1. Before that he's only mentioned ("Alvin's in Miami").
- **Theodore, Connie, and Reginald Donohue** first appear in **Episodes #176–180 (September 12–16, 2005)**, the final week of Season 1. They are **promoted to Core Contract Cast for Season 2.**
  - Before that, Theodore exists only as a written proxy vote (Episode #90) and as the unheard person on the other end of Martin's one-sided calls.
  - Neither counts as an appearance.
- **Child performers** (Emma, Victoria, Lyric) have contracts, but their counts are limited by child-labor hour rules.

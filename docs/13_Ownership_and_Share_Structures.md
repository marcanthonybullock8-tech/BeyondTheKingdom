# BEYOND THE KINGDOM — Ownership & Share Structures

**Document 13 · Locked Canon · Who Owns What (as of Monday, January 3, 2005)**
**Donohue Global Holdings · Donohue Enterprises · Smilley International**

---

## Why These Structures Are Realistic

Real family empires almost never hold 100% of their companies outright. They split **voting control** (who decides) from **economic ownership** (who gets the money). *Beyond the Kingdom* uses structures modeled on real companies:

| Real-World Model | What It Does | Where We Use It |
|---|---|---|
| **Robert Bosch GmbH** (Germany) | A family trust holds ~93% of the votes but almost none of the equity. A foundation holds most of the economic value. | **The Crown Trust:** 100% of DGH's votes with only 2% of its value |
| **Tata Sons** (India) | A private holding company owns controlling stakes in separately listed operating companies | **DGH's controlling stakes** in its divisions |
| **Cargill / Mars** (U.S.) | Private, multi-generational family trusts plus employee ownership | **Dynasty trusts** for each branch, plus the **Alexander Share Plan** |
| **Dual-class family companies** (e.g., newspaper dynasties) | Class A voting shares for the family, Class B economic shares for everyone else | **Smilley International** |

**The one rule to remember:** *Votes are power. Shares are money.* Many of the season's fights come from characters who have one and want the other.

## Voting Control — 100% the Crown Trust

- **The Crown Trust** (the Alexander & Simone Donohue Family Trust, created March 1, 1971) owns **all 2,000,000 Class A shares.** Every DGH vote is the Trust's vote.
- The Trust is directed by the **Crown Council** (nine seats; see Document 03). Nobody, not even Victor, can sell, merge, or move DGH without the Council.
- **This is why the Disgrace Clause matters.** The Heir Apparent eventually leads the Council, and **whoever leads the Council controls the most valuable company on Earth**, no matter how few shares they personally own.

# PART TWO — DONOHUE ENTERPRISES, INC.

**Donohue Enterprises** is the **operating empire**, the management company that runs the day-to-day businesses. It was incorporated **November 2, 1931**, and has been owned by DGH since 1966.

## Share Structure

| Holder | Class | % of Votes | % of Economics | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Donohue Global Holdings** | Common | **100%** | **95.0%** | Wholly controlled subsidiary |
| **Enterprise Partners' Plan** | Non-voting profit units | 0% | **4.5%** | Incentive equity for about 400 senior executives: division presidents, the C-suite, and Natasha as CEO. Vests over 10 years. |
| **The Bellmen's Trust** | Non-voting | 0% | **0.5%** | In 1936 Alexander gave shares to the original staff of the Hotel Donohue (bellmen, cooks, maids). Their grandchildren still receive checks. |
| **Certificate No. 1** (the Founder's Share) | 1 golden share | **Veto** | — | See below |

### Certificate No. 1 — The Golden Share
- This is **Donohue Enterprises' original 1931 share certificate**, signed by Alexander and kept framed in the Dowager House.
- **It carries one power: a veto** over any sale or merger of Donohue Enterprises, and over **moving its headquarters out of Atlanta.**
- **Holder:** Simone Donohue. **On her death, it passes to the Heir Apparent.**
- *Story use:* It's the one thing the Smilleys can never buy, and the one thing Theodore would need if he ever tried to sell off the empire.

### The Voting Math (the Smilley Soap Engine)
- **Naming a CEO requires a majority (over 50%) of Class A votes.** Lucius is Chairman & CEO today, but **if Lucius is incapacitated or dies, the family votes on his successor.**
- **Esther's bloc:** Lucius (34) + Esther (12) = **46%. Not enough.**
- **Desmond's bloc** (once Luke learns Desmond is his father in May): Desmond (16) + Luke (6) + Delphine (6) + Quentin (2) = **30%**. **Add Vivienne (4) and it's 34%.**
- **The kingmaker: Odessa (20%).**
  - If Odessa backs Esther, Esther wins with **66%**.
  - If Odessa backs Desmond, Desmond wins with **54%**.
- **So the 84-year-old grandmother holds the fate of a $1.9 trillion company**, and she's the woman who reads the Savannah Letter and starts making peace with Simone Donohue. *(Season 1 seeds. Season 2 pays it off.)*
- **The Episode #103 directive:** After his collapse, Lucius **rewrites his will** so that all 34% of his votes go to **Esther** when he dies. That would give her 46% alone, still short of a majority without Odessa. **Desmond's only move is to force a vote before Lucius dies.**
- **Quentin's 2%** looks tiny until the vote is close. Then Mallory's boyfriend becomes the most courted man in Atlanta.

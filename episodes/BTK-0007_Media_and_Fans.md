## DOW JONES NEWSWIRES

*10:02 a.m. EST, Tuesday, January 11, 2005*

> **SMILLEY INTERNATIONAL NAMES EXCLUSIVE ATLANTA BUYER'S BROKER FOR $2 BLN REAL ESTATE PROGRAM**

ATLANTA (Dow Jones) — Smilley International Inc., the Chicago-founded consumer and hospitality conglomerate that moved its headquarters to Atlanta this month, said Tuesday it will deploy up to $2 billion in Atlanta-area real estate over the next 12 months.

The privately held company named Harmony Divine, 22, of Keel & Associates Realty, Buckhead, as its exclusive buyer's representative for the program. Terms weren't disclosed.

A company spokeswoman said the program will include office, retail, residential and land acquisitions "across the urban core."

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Wednesday, January 12, 2005 · 50¢

### BUSINESS · D1

> **SMILLEYS BET $2 BILLION ON ATLANTA, AND ON A 22-YEAR-OLD**
> *Little-known broker lands one of the largest buy-side mandates in city history*

*By Nathan Greenlee, Business Writer*

Until Tuesday morning, Harmony Divine was best known in Atlanta real estate circles, if at all, for moving starter condos in Lindbergh and Grant Park. By lunchtime, she held a mandate that veteran commercial brokers called "unprecedented" and "frankly insulting."

"There are forty brokers in this city who have done nine-figure deals," said one, who asked not to be named because he hopes to do business with Smilley International. "They picked a kid."

Industry sources estimate that a customary buy-side fee of 1 to 1.5 percent on $2 billion in closed transactions would total $20 million to $30 million.

Divine and Esther Smilley, president of Smilley International Atlanta, were roommates at Northwestern University. Divine declined to comment. Keel & Associates broker Donna Keel said the firm was "thrilled and not at all surprised."

Donohue Global Holdings declined to comment.

### LIVING · "BEHIND THE GATES" by Bernadette Sinclair

**The Girl in the Navy Suit.**

Darlings, the valet at Smilley Tower tells me that at 8:51 Tuesday morning, a dented silver Honda Civic pulled up in a line of Bentleys, and a young woman in a **navy St. John** handed him the keys and said, "It pulls a little to the left."

By 9:48 she was the most important real estate agent in Atlanta.

I have been asked all day who **Harmony Divine** is. I will tell you. She is the daughter of a Grady nurse and a MARTA driver on Route 71. She is the sister of a young man who walked off Northside Drive on New Year's morning three years ago and never came home. And she is, I am told, the best friend of the most powerful 22-year-old woman in the South.

In this town, darling, that is a résumé.

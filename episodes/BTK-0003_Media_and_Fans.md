## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Thursday, January 6, 2005 · Home Edition · 50¢

### FRONT PAGE

> **WEST END ON EDGE AHEAD OF PARK VICTIM'S FUNERAL**
> *Whitlock to be buried Saturday; unmarked police car posted outside Merriweather & Sons*

*By Marcus Webb, Staff Writer*

Lamar Whitlock, the 43-year-old West End man found shot to death in Piedmont Park on Monday, will be buried Saturday morning after an 11 a.m. service at Greater Cascade Baptist Church, his family announced Wednesday.

An unmarked Atlanta police car was seen parked across from Merriweather & Sons Funeral Home on Ralph David Abernathy Boulevard throughout the day Wednesday. The funeral home, which employed Whitlock part-time, is handling the arrangements. An APD spokesperson said only that "the department maintains a presence wherever community safety requires it."

Asked about reports that Deputy Chief Warren Hughes had objected to the cost of the surveillance, the spokesperson declined to comment. *(Continued on A6)*

> **DONOHUE HEIR TURNS 23; KEY TO 1936 HOTEL CHANGES HANDS**

In a quiet ritual that DGH insiders say dates back more than a decade, Chairman Victor Donohue brought his grandson, Blackwater Security International founder Marc-Anthony Bullock, to the shuttered Hotel Donohue on Auburn Avenue at dawn Wednesday, his 23rd birthday. Real estate analysts noted that the visit came as Smilley Estates continues to acquire options on Sweet Auburn parcels. "If you're the Donohues, you make sure the whole city knows who holds the key to that street," said one Midtown developer. "Literally."

### LIVING · "BEHIND THE GATES" by Bernadette Sinclair

**The Birthday. Darlings, I need to lie down.**

I have attended forty years of Belmont Crest parties, and I have never seen three hundred people cry at the same time until Wednesday night at **Belle Rive**.

**The Watch.** At ten o'clock, **Simone Donohue**, 94, placed her late husband's 1936 gold pocket watch in the palm of her great-grandson, **Marc-Anthony Bullock**, and told him, "This was his. He'd want it ticking." I watched a sitting federal judge dab her eyes with a cocktail napkin. I watched a Georgia state senator blow his nose into a pocket square that cost more than my first car. I watched *myself* do something similar.

**The Song.** **Grace Bullock** performed "Crown Me" with a brand-new second verse written for her brother. I won't print the lyrics. I'll just tell you the organist from Greater Crest Tabernacle said "Amen" out loud.

**The Gifts.** A 1964 Patek Philippe from the parents. A 1969 Harley from an uncle who is still, we're told, "in Miami." And from cousin **Martin Olson**, a 1640 first edition of Machiavelli's *The Prince*, inscribed. The birthday boy thanked him graciously and then reminded the room, charmingly, that Machiavelli wrote it after being tortured. The room laughed. Cousin Martin laughed a half-second late.

**The Duck.** It's real. It's a swan in a crown, and it looks exactly like a duck that won the lottery. The CEO of Donohue Global Holdings ordered it lit "like a Broadway star." It was. Truly one of the great ducks.

**Sightings:**
- **Mayor Josephine Whitaker**, in sequins, spent a *very* cozy ten minutes in a certain study with the **Chairman**. Donation talk, surely.
- **Chief Elxa Jackson**, in simple black, ate shrimp standing up. The Chief never eats standing up.
- A slim young woman in a **black hoodie** was seen communing silently with the duck before vanishing with a slice of cake. Security, I'm told. Darling, I *love* this family's security.

**And finally, a blind item that is not blind at all.** At approximately 9:41 PM, a black **Maybach** bearing a certain Chicago family's vanity plate pulled up to the **Crown Gate**. The rear window lowered. My source saw **scarlet silk**. The guard picked up his radio to announce her.

And then the car **turned around.**

Two visits to one gate in three days, darlings. I'm no detective, but I've been to a few weddings.

## "FIND DEE" — finddee.livejournal.com

**Wednesday, January 5th, 2005 — 11:58 pm**
*Current mood:* hopeful
*Current music:* Grace Bullock, "Crown Me"

**I met the DA tonight.**

At a party. A very fancy party. Lyric's godfather turned 23, and he made me come, and I wore my cousin's dress and felt like Cinderella if Cinderella had student loans.

And the new District Attorney was there. Arianna Cummings. I made a fool of myself. I just started talking about Dee right in the middle of a birthday party, and I was so embarrassed.

But she was so kind. She said she's seen the flyers. The yellow ones. She said, "Come see me tomorrow. Ten o'clock."

Three years. Three years of detectives not calling back and flyers getting rained on. And the District Attorney of Fulton County looked me in the eye and said, "Come see me."

She looked like she'd seen a ghost when I said his name, though. I guess everybody does now. It's been so long.

10 AM. Wish me luck. Pray for me. Pray for Dee.

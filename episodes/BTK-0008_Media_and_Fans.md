## WBKG CHANNEL 8 ACTION NEWS — WBKG.com

*Posted 12:40 p.m., Wednesday, January 12, 2005*

> **CHIEF FIRES BACK AT COUNCIL HEARING: 'HE'S VERY MUCH IN THE LOOP'**

A routine city budget hearing turned tense Wednesday morning when Deputy Chief Warren Hughes told the Atlanta City Council he was "not in the loop" on the police department's Blackwater Task Force.

Chief Elxa Jackson, watching from the gallery, rose to answer Councilman Leon Hardaway's question about the task force's cost herself: $118,000 a month, offset, she said, by ending an overtime detail at the Governor's Mansion "that nobody in this building has been able to justify since 1998."

"Deputy Chief Hughes is very much in the loop," the Chief said. "He'll be at my table tomorrow morning at nine, with everybody else."

The Governor's office did not return a call seeking comment.

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Thursday, January 13, 2005 · 50¢

### METRO · B1

> **HUGHES, JACKSON SPAR AT COUNCIL; TASK FORCE COST REVEALED**

*By Renee Holloway, Staff Writer*

The simmering tension between Atlanta's 24-year-old police chief and the veteran deputy many expected to get her job spilled into public view Wednesday.

"The Chief keeps a small table," Deputy Chief Warren Hughes, a 25-year APD veteran, told the Council. "I'm sure she has her reasons."

Council members from both factions praised the Chief's answer afterward. "That," said Councilman Leon Hardaway, "is how you answer a question."

The department's command staff meets this morning to review the Blackwater investigation.

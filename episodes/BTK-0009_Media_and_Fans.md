## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Friday, January 14, 2005 · 50¢

### FRONT PAGE

> **'FAKE COIN' LEAK ROCKS APD**
> *Chief's private theory reaches TV within an hour of closed-door meeting*

*By Renee Holloway, Staff Writer*

A confidential assessment by Police Chief Elxa Jackson, that the "Onyx Coin" killings may be the work of someone framing the Blackwater Syndicate, was broadcast Thursday morning less than half an hour after the department's top commanders reviewed it behind closed doors.

The leak comes one day after Deputy Chief Warren Hughes told the City Council he was "not in the loop" on the Blackwater investigation.

On Thursday evening, the department announced that Hughes has been named APD's liaison to a federal organized-crime task force. A spokeswoman called it "a routine assignment that reflects the Deputy Chief's experience."

"That's either the dumbest thing I've ever seen a chief do or the smartest," said one retired APD commander. "Ask me in a month."

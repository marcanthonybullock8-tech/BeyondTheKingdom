## WBKG CHANNEL 8 ACTION NEWS — WBKG.com

*Posted 5:48 p.m., Friday, January 14, 2005*

> **FBI LAB: 'ONYX COIN' STONE TRACED TO SOUTH AFRICA**

The black onyx coin found with Piedmont Park victim Lamar Whitlock was cut from stone mined in South Africa, according to an FBI laboratory analysis shared with the Atlanta Police Department on Friday, two law enforcement sources told Channel 8.

The finding appears to support Chief Elxa Jackson's reported belief that the coins are counterfeits. APD declined to comment.

Deputy Chief Warren Hughes, APD's new liaison to the FBI, was seen leaving the Bureau's Century Parkway field office Friday morning. Reached by phone, Hughes said only: "Ask the Chief. It's her case."

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Saturday, January 15, 2005 · Weekend Edition · 75¢

### FRONT PAGE

> **'MADE IN SOUTH AFRICA': FEDS, APD CLOSE RANKS ON COIN KILLINGS**
> *Chief and FBI agent seen together as lab links fake coin to foreign stone*

*By Renee Holloway, Staff Writer*

A week after federal agents took custody of the "Onyx Coin" evidence under a court order, Atlanta's police chief and the FBI's lead agent on the case appear to have put their public feud aside.

Chief Elxa Jackson and Special Agent Kathleen Doyle were seen sharing a back booth at Manuel's Tavern on North Highland Avenue on Friday evening. Neither would comment on the meeting. A regular at the bar said the Chief "drank a Coca-Cola and left a twenty-dollar tip."

### BUSINESS · D1

> **SMILLEYS MAKE FIRST MOVE ON SWEET AUBURN**

Smilley International has secured an exclusive option on two parcels at 441 and 443 Auburn Avenue, steps from the historic Hotel Donohue, according to people familiar with the transaction. The deal was brokered by Harmony Divine of Keel & Associates, the 22-year-old named this week to lead the company's $2 billion Atlanta acquisition program.

City Hall has not confirmed long-rumored plans for a major Sweet Auburn redevelopment.

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Wednesday, January 5, 2005 · Home Edition · 50¢

### FRONT PAGE

> **PARK VICTIM IDENTIFIED AS WEST END MAN, 43**
> *Police silent on reported 'Blackwater' coin; funeral home owner calls slain employee "a deacon"*

*By Marcus Webb, Staff Writer*

The man found shot to death Monday night beside Lake Clara Meer in Piedmont Park was Lamar Whitlock, 43, of the West End, Atlanta police said at a brief news conference Tuesday evening.

Whitlock had two prior convictions for commercial gambling and one for receiving stolen property, according to state records. Silas Merriweather, owner of Merriweather & Sons Funeral Homes, said in a written statement that Whitlock was "a part-time driver, a deacon, and a beloved son of the West End," and that his firm would pay for the funeral.

Police would not confirm published reports, first aired by WBKG Channel 8, that the victim was found holding a black onyx coin associated with the criminal organization known as the Blackwater Syndicate. Deputy Chief Warren Hughes, speaking at the briefing, said only that "all avenues are being explored."

Chief Elxa Jackson did not appear at the briefing.

West End residents said Tuesday they feared the killing could reignite the wave of unexplained fires that struck Southside businesses last summer. "Everybody's holding their breath," said one restaurant worker on Ralph David Abernathy Boulevard, who asked not to be named. "Nobody wants another summer like that." *(Continued on A6)*

> **OLSON SWORN IN FOR 10TH TERM**
> *Congresswoman, 44, is now among the longest-serving women in Georgia's history*

*By Deborah Kane, Washington Bureau*

WASHINGTON — Rep. Jasmine Donohue Olson (D-Ga.) took the oath of office Tuesday for her 10th consecutive term. She is the only member of the Georgia delegation first elected before the age of 30.

"Ten terms, and I still get butterflies," Olson told reporters in Statuary Hall. Asked about persistent speculation that she will run for statewide office in 2006, she smiled and said: "Today I'm running to the Ways and Means Committee room. That's as far as I've planned."

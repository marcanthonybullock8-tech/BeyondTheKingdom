## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Friday, January 7, 2005 · Home Edition · 50¢

*(Went to press at 11:30 PM Thursday.)*

### METRO · B1

> **NEW DA REVIVES COLD CASE UNIT: '640 FAMILIES ARE WAITING'**
> *Cummings restaffs long-dormant unit; four investigators start Monday*

*By Tamara Ellison, Courts Reporter*

Fulton County District Attorney Arianna Cummings, sworn in last Saturday, has restaffed the county's long-dormant Cold Case Unit with four veteran investigators, her office confirmed Thursday.

The unit had been reduced to "two investigators and a closet" under former DA Lawrence Tate, according to a staff memo obtained by the *Sentinel*. It holds 640 unsolved homicides and missing-person cases dating back to 1998.

"Every family who's been waiting will get a phone call by the end of the month," Cummings said in a brief statement. "Waiting is the cruelest sentence there is."

Among the families who met with the DA on Thursday, according to courthouse staff, was the family of Darnell Divine, a Cascade Heights barber missing since New Year's Eve 2001.

> **MORTICIAN SERVES COFFEE TO POLICE STAKEOUT**
> *'Nothing to hide,' says Merriweather*

*By Marcus Webb, Staff Writer*

Plainclothes officers posted outside Merriweather & Sons Funeral Home on Ralph David Abernathy Boulevard received an unexpected delivery Thursday morning: two cups of coffee and a dozen cream-filled doughnuts, courtesy of the owner.

"Mr. Merriweather has nothing to hide and nobody to fear except whoever killed Lamar Whitlock," said an employee who made the delivery. APD declined to comment on the surveillance, or on the doughnuts.

## "FIND DEE" — finddee.livejournal.com

**Thursday, January 6th, 2005 — 9:48 pm**
*Current mood:* overwhelmed

**I met with the District Attorney today.**

Mama came with me, straight off a twelve-hour shift. I brought the binder. She'd already read the file before we got there. At seven in the morning.

She told us a detective wrote "no leads" after NINE DAYS. Nine days, y'all. We've been looking for three years.

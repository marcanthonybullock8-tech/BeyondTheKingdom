> **RIVAL GANGS STAND TOGETHER AT SLAIN DEACON'S FUNERAL**

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Saturday, January 8, 2005 · Weekend Edition · Metro B1

> **NEW DA REOPENS CASE OF MISSING SOUTHWEST ATLANTA MAN**
> *Darnell Divine vanished New Year's Eve 2001; family's online campaign cited*

*By Renee Holloway, Staff Writer*

Fulton County District Attorney Arianna Cummings has reopened the three-year-old missing-person case of Darnell "Dee" Divine, 22 at the time of his disappearance, and assigned it to the office's Cold Case Unit, a spokesman confirmed Friday.

Divine was last seen leaving a private party on the 3100 block of Northside Drive NW early on January 1, 2002. The Atlanta Police Department classified the case as "inactive" nine days later.

His sister, Harmony Divine, 22, has kept a public online journal, "Find Dee," every week since. In a Friday post she thanked the District Attorney by name. "For the first time in three years, I believe somebody," she wrote.

The case has been assigned to investigator Carla Mims, a 22-year veteran of the Georgia Bureau of Investigation. *(Continued on B4)*

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Tuesday, January 11, 2005 · 50¢

### METRO · B1

> **'NORTHSIDE DRIVE, NEW YEAR'S EVE': DA ASKS BUCKHEAD HOSTS FOR 2001 GUEST LISTS**
> *Cold-case investigator seeks witnesses who may have seen missing man walking*

*By Renee Holloway, Staff Writer*

Letters signed by District Attorney Arianna Cummings went out Monday to homeowners on Northside Drive NW who hosted private gatherings on New Year's Eve 2001, asking them to share guest lists and the names of caterers, valets, and security contractors.

"Somebody saw a young man walking," investigator Carla Mims said. "Somebody always sees. They just never knew it mattered."

At least one recipient, who asked not to be named, called the request "unusual" but said she would comply. "It's the DA," she said. "You don't say no to the DA in her first month."

## THE ATLANTA SENTINEL — Tuesday, January 4, 2005 · Home Edition · 50¢

### FRONT PAGE

> **BODY IN PIEDMONT PARK CLUTCHED 'BLACKWATER' COIN**
> *Victim unidentified; new Chief vowed hours earlier to catch syndicate's leader*

*By Marcus Webb, Staff Writer · Filed 11:52 p.m.*

A man found shot to death beside Lake Clara Meer in Piedmont Park late Monday was holding a black onyx coin engraved with a crown and a wave, the symbol associated with the criminal organization known as the Blackwater Syndicate, according to two sources familiar with the investigation.

The man, described as a Black male in his early 40s, carried no identification. A dog walker found him at about 9:50 p.m. Police did not confirm the coin's existence. Police Chief Elxa Jackson, who arrived at the scene in dress uniform, declined to comment.

The discovery came less than 11 hours after Jackson, 24, used her first press conference of 2005 to promise that her new task force would identify the Syndicate's leader, known only as "Sovereign." *(Continued on A6)*

---

> **SMILLEY TOWER RISES ON PEACHTREE**
> *Chicago dynasty opens 72-story headquarters directly across from Donohue Tower. "I don't declare war. I declare dividends."*

*By Lorraine Figueroa, Business Staff*

At 7:42 a.m. Monday, as the sun came up over Buckhead, 72 floors of silver glass lit up one after another. By 10 a.m. Smilley International had cut the ribbon on its new world headquarters at 3434 Peachtree Road NE, which faces Donohue Tower across six lanes of traffic.

"In 1926, my father was not allowed to get off the train in this city," Chairman Lucius Smilley told a crowd of 300 that included Mayor Josephine Whitaker. "Seventy-nine years later, we brought the station."

The ribbon was cut by Esther Smilley, 22, the company's designated heir. She oversaw the tower's construction in secret through a shell company. Asked if the move was a declaration of war on the Donohue family, she replied: "I don't declare war. I declare dividends."

A spokesperson for Donohue Global Holdings declined to comment. *(Continued on D1)*

### LIVING · "BEHIND THE GATES" by Bernadette Sinclair

**Darlings, the neighbors have arrived.**

Monday was the most elegant hostile takeover of a news cycle I have seen in thirty years. The Smilleys lit their tower *at sunrise*, just to make sure one particular early riser eighty-eight floors up would see it with his coffee.

**Sightings:**
- **Odessa Smilley**, 84 going on 50, in a floor-length white mink and a diamond brooch I could see from the parking deck. She demanded the Smilley Tower elevators stop playing Kenny G. A woman of taste.
- **Esther Smilley**, stunning in black cashmere, silencing a certain Channel 8 reporter with a single line about her daughters' "father." More on that shortly.
- **Quentin Smilley**, arriving at a ribbon-cutting in last night's velvet. Welcome to Atlanta, sugar. We have church here.

**Blind item:** Which newly arrived heiress had her Maybach *idle outside a certain crowned gate* for nearly a minute Monday morning before speeding away? Curiosity, or reconnaissance?

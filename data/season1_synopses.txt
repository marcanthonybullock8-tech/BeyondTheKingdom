**THE RETURN.** A black Maybach stops at the Crown Gate, and for the first time in two years the lights come on at Ashgrove. **Theodore Donohue** steps out, home from Monaco with his son Reginald. Victor: "Hello, brother."
Theodore greets the family with smiles and gifts, and **Connie Donohue** makes her grand entrance. Privately, Theodore and Martin confirm their plan. Simone studies her eldest son: "You came home for a funeral. Whose?"
At the rehearsal dinner, Esther asks Marc: "Give me one reason not to marry him." Marc gives her four years of reasons, and she walks away in tears. Theodore calls an emergency Crown Council for October.
The audience learns that Connie is CrestWatch as she posts "WEDDING OF THE CENTURY, OR A FUNERAL?" Arianna mails her confession to Harmony by overnight courier.
**SEASON FINALE.** Esther walks down the aisle at Ravenhurst. When the officiant asks for objections, three-year-old Victoria runs to Marc crying "I want my Marc!" Harmony reads Arianna's letter and turns to stare at Amond, and Martin whispers to Theodore, "I know who Sovereign is, and whose daughters those are." As Esther opens her mouth to answer, shots ring out from the tree line. Marc throws himself over Esther and the twins, and as blood spreads across her white gown, Esther screams: **"Marc, stay with me! They're yours! The girls are yours!"** *Fade to black.*
